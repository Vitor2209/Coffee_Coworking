About Montis

Our story, vision, and values.

The Beginning

Our Story

Montis Coffee House was born from a simple idea: to bring the tranquility of Brazilian mountain coffee farms to the urban rhythm of London.

The name "Montis" comes from Latin for mountain, reflecting our connection to the highland coffee regions of Minas Gerais, Brazil.

At altitudes above 1,000 metres, coffee beans develop slowly, producing complex flavours and natural sweetness that define the Montis experience.

Our Vision

"A sanctuary where exceptional coffee meets thoughtful workspace design."

We envisioned a space where professionals could find calm amidst the city—where focus thrives and creativity flows.
